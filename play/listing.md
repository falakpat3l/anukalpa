## Store listing

App name (24/30):
anukalpa: Generic Finder

Short description (75/80):
Find the generic name behind a medicine brand. Offline, no ads, no account.

Full description (1170/4000):

anukalpa (Sanskrit for "an alternative") shows you the generic name to ask for behind a medicine brand, and other brands with the same salts and strength.

Type a brand or a salt, for example Dolo 650, Pan 40 or metformin, and see:
- The generic name to ask for, with salts and strength
- Other brands with exactly the same salts and strength
- Results for India, the US, Canada and the EU, or all of them together

Useful with Jan Aushadhi and other generic medicine stores, or when your usual brand is out of stock.

Private by design
- Works fully offline: every list is inside the app
- No internet permission, no account, no ads, no tracking
- Recent searches stay only on your phone, and Clear removes them

Where the data comes from
India: the Drug Information Service Bundle by C-DAC / NRCeS (CC BY 4.0). US: FDA NDC directory. Canada: Health Canada Drug Product Database. EU: European Medicines Agency list. Brand names belong to their owners.

For information only. Do not change or stop a medicine without asking your doctor or pharmacist. Brands with the same salts can still differ in form or release.

Made by Falak Patel, a pharmacist in Bharuch, Gujarat.
