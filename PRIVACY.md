# anukalpa privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

anukalpa is an Android app made by Falak Patel.

## In short

anukalpa does not collect, send or share any of your data. Everything stays on your phone.

## What the app keeps on your phone

- Your last 5 searches (shown under the search box; Clear removes them)

This is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## No internet, no tracking

anukalpa has no internet permission, so it cannot send data off your phone. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising ID. Links in the app (for example to the data sources) open in your browser.

## Permissions

- None. The app asks for no permissions at all.

## Children

The app is not made for children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
